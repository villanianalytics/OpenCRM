# Code of Conduct

OpenCRM contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and harassment-free.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, accept good-faith feedback, protect private information, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or abusive conduct. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `dvillani@rapidanalyticsinc.com`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially to the extent practical. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
